Brand artwork bundled with NetworkMapper

Simple Icons 16.0.0
Source: https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/16.0.0/icons
Project license: https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/16.0.0/LICENSE.md

Dashboard Icons
Source: https://github.com/homarr-labs/dashboard-icons/tree/59f1e9a4108f4f510f41e9588f078631de1e1405/png

to their respective owners. Consult the brand's usage guidance before
redistributing the application or publishing a map that includes its mark.
